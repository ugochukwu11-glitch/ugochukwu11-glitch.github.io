Customer Churn Prediction

An end-to-end machine learning system designed to predict customer churn and enable proactive retention decisions.

📋

Project Overview

This project focuses on predicting customer churn using structured telecom data. The objective extends beyond classification to delivering risk-aware insights that can support real-world business decisions.

Impact: Enables targeted retention strategies using predictive insights.

🧠

Decision Rationale

  • Framed churn as a risk prediction problem, not mere classification.
  • Prioritized recall and probability outputs over raw accuracy.
  • Selected Random Forest to balance performance, robustness, and explainability.
  • Avoided aggressive feature engineering to preserve real behavior signals.
  • Designed the system as a deployable data product.
🧩

Full Thought Process

Click to expand full reasoning and methodology

1. Framing the Problem

The goal was to predict churn early enough to enable intervention. Probability outputs were favored to support risk-based decision-making.

2. Understanding the Data

The dataset was examined conceptually before preprocessing to understand behavioral, demographic, and lifecycle signals.

3. Data Cleaning Philosophy

Cleaning focused on correcting inconsistencies while preserving real-world meaning.

4. Exploratory Data Analysis

EDA acted as hypothesis testing: tenure, contract type, and pricing patterns all aligned strongly with churn behavior.

5. Feature Engineering Strategy

Feature engineering was intentionally minimal to avoid noise amplification.

6. Model Selection

Logistic Regression served as a baseline, Random Forest was selected for deployment due to stability and interpretability.

7. Evaluation Philosophy

Accuracy alone was rejected; recall and ROC-AUC guided selection.

8. Explainability

Feature importance aligned with intuition and improved stakeholder trust.

9. Deployment Thinking

The model was separated from inference and exposed via a Streamlit dashboard.

10. Reflection

The project favors explainability, reliability, and business relevance over raw optimization.

🎯

Problem Definition

Subscription businesses face revenue loss due to customer churn. Predicting churn enables proactive retention rather than reactive recovery.
