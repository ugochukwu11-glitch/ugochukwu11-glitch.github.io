Projects

Employee Attrition Analysis

Problem:
Understanding the factors contributing to employee attrition in an organization using a small, imperfect HR dataset.

Data:
A dataset containing employee demographics, job roles, satisfaction levels, compensation, and tenure, with missing values and inconsistent formats.

Approach:
Performed exploratory data analysis, cleaned and standardized features, engineered relevant variables, and evaluated classification models to identify key attrition drivers.

Key Insights:
Employee satisfaction and compensation bands showed the strongest association with attrition, suggesting retention efforts should focus on role alignment and pay equity rather than tenure alone.

Tools: Python, Pandas, SQL
