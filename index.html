About Me

I am a Data Scientist focused on turning real-world, often messy data into insights that support better decisions. I care less about chasing complexity for its own sake and more about building analyses and models that people can actually understand, trust, and use.

My work usually begins long before modeling — with data cleaning, exploration, and asking the right questions. I enjoy breaking problems down, identifying what truly matters, and communicating findings clearly and honestly.

I have worked across analytics, SQL-based exploration, automation workflows, and applied machine learning. I see these not as isolated skills, but as tools within a broader problem-solving process aimed at reducing uncertainty.

I value clarity, structure, and thoughtful execution. I prefer progress over perfection, and explanations over assumptions.
